# Adversarial FAQ

Common misinterpretations and why they are wrong.

---

### 1. "This MTB proves the content is correct."

**Incorrect.**
The MTB proves that specific assets existed in a specific form at a specific time and passed specific policy checks. It does not assert factual correctness, quality, or fitness for purpose.

---

### 2. "MK10-PRO guarantees delivery quality."

**Incorrect.**
MK10-PRO records state and policy compliance. It does not evaluate artistic quality, technical excellence, or suitability for any particular use case.

---

### 3. "A corrected MTB can replace an earlier one."

**Incorrect.**
Each MTB is an independent record. A new MTB from corrected assets does not invalidate earlier MTBs. Both remain valid records of their respective states.

---

### 4. "MK10-PRO assumes responsibility for the decision."

**Incorrect.**
Responsibility remains with the human party who prepared the assets, ran the verification, and interpreted the result. The system computes deterministically; it does not judge, decide, or assume liability.

---

### 5. "Different hashes mean the system is unreliable."

**Incorrect.**
MK10-PRO is deterministic. Different hashes necessarily imply different inputs, even if the difference is a single byte. Only if byte-identical inputs provably produce different hashes would this indicate a system failure.

---

### 6. "The MTB is legally binding by itself."

**Incorrect.**
Admissibility and legal effect are determined by courts or contractual agreements. The MTB provides cryptographic proof of state, not automatic legal authority or enforceability.

---

### 7. "Using the MTB removes the need for human review."

**Incorrect.**
An MTB without understanding the underlying assets and policy semantics is meaningless. The system is a precision tool; safe use requires informed human interpretation.
